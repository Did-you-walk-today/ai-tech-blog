Json House is a structured-data resource for AI models, developer tools, prompt engineering, and AI productivity. Its guiding value is **structured data you can actually use**: every post is written for people and ships with a machine-readable dataset, so a human reader and an AI agent can extract the same facts.

## What we publish

- **Posts** at `/posts/{slug}/`: comparisons, pricing tables, benchmarks, and analysis, each with the sources behind its numbers.
- **Datasets** at `/data/{slug}.json`: the key facts, comparison tables, and primary sources of each post. The full list is in [/data/index.json](/data/index.json).
- **Indexes for machines**: [/api/posts.json](/api/posts.json) lists every post, and [/llms.txt](/llms.txt) is a plain-text map of the site.

## How we work

Pricing, model specs, and benchmark scores come from primary sources such as vendor pricing pages, official documentation, and release notes. Data posts state their own limits, and when we get something wrong we correct the post and note the change in its changelog. The full standard is on the [Editorial Methodology](/methodology/) page, and the terms for reusing our datasets are in the [Data Use Policy](/data-policy/).

Prices and specs in this field change often. Confirm a figure against the linked primary source before you rely on it.
